# Diagnostic Topology Findings

Status: Working draft

Purpose:
Track evidence that EDF analyses may exhibit recurring structural patterns.

Important:
Diagnostic topology is not yet part of EDF.
It is a research hypothesis.

## Current Observed Patterns

| Validation Case | Outcome Type | Observed Structure |
|---|---|---|
| Challenger | Failure | Integrated sociotechnical / cross-context breakdown |
| Apollo 13 | Failure and recovery | Cyclic adaptive recovery / evolving diagnostic state |

## Research Question RQ-001

Do Validation Cases naturally exhibit recurring system behaviors or diagnostic topologies that improve diagnosis, synthesis, or intervention if explicitly identified?

## Research Question RQ-003

Do origin networks naturally organize into interacting layers such as technical, organizational, informational, operational, environmental, and human?

## Research Question RQ-006

Are sustained excellence and resilience distinct recurring system behaviors, or are they manifestations of a broader adaptive system pattern?

## Caution

Do not add these as EDF fields yet.

Diagnostic Topology is still not accepted into EDF.

Layered Origin Networks are not accepted into EDF.

These remain research hypotheses.

Do not add system behavior or diagnostic topology to EDF yet.

Chernobyl is planned as a Longitudinal Reference Case and should not initially be forced into an existing topology. Its primary purpose is to test evidence evolution and Diagnostic Stability.

## Hypothesis

System behavior or diagnostic topology may be a property derived from a completed EDF analysis, not an input field required at the beginning.

## Acceptance Criteria Before Adding Anything To EDF

- Improves at least three Validation Cases.
- Improves or does not complicate at least one EDF-0 simple case.
- Improves control-point selection.
- Improves Diagnostic Calibration or synthesis.
- Can be explained clearly in one paragraph.
- Satisfies Concept Burden and the EDF Constitution.

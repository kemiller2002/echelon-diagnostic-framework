# CFCV-002 - FMEA

Status: Working draft

Use case:
Chernobyl modern evidence state.

## Summary

FMEA asks:

- How can this system fail?
- What happens if it does?
- How severe is the consequence?
- How likely is it?
- How likely are we to detect it?
- Which risks should be reduced first?

## Finding

FMEA is primarily prospective.

EDF is primarily diagnostic / retrospective, though it can inform future control points.

FMEA is stronger at:

- Prevention
- Design review
- Risk prioritization
- Engineering reliability work

EDF is stronger at:

- Historical diagnosis
- Complex sociotechnical systems
- System Context
- Origin interactions
- Evidence evolution
- Governance integration
- Broad control-point identification

## Comparison

| Capability | FMEA | EDF | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Prevention | Excellent | Moderate | FMEA |
| Design review | Excellent | Moderate | FMEA |
| Risk prioritization | Excellent | Limited | FMEA |
| Historical diagnosis | Moderate | Excellent | EDF |
| Complex sociotechnical systems | Moderate | Excellent | EDF |
| System Context | Moderate | Excellent | EDF |
| Origin interactions | Moderate | Excellent | EDF |
| Evidence evolution | Weak | Strong | EDF |
| Governance integration | Limited | Strong | EDF |

## Conclusion

FMEA and EDF are complementary.

FMEA asks:

> What could fail?

EDF asks:

> What produced this outcome?

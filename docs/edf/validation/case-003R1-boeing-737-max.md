# Validation Case 003R1 - Boeing 737 MAX

Status: Working draft

- FCR: FCR-000003
- EDF: v0.3 frozen
- Validation Protocol: v1.1
- Analytical Lens Set: v1.0

## Purpose

Re-test Boeing under v1.1 and evaluate reproducibility.

## Historical Baseline

The 737 MAX accidents involved interacting technical, organizational, certification, and communication failures rather than a single isolated defect.

## Natural Analysis Summary

- Systems Lens: Flight control architecture, sensor dependencies, software assumptions, human-machine interfaces, certification interfaces, organizational feedback loops
- Governance Lens: Market pressures, schedule pressure, incentives, certification strategy, safety governance
- Investigation Lens: Evidence, assumptions, warning signs, ignored competing explanations
- Differential Diagnosis Lens: Progressive deterioration of safety margins
- Legal / Evidentiary Lens: Certification evidence, documentation, disclosure, accountability

## EDF Analysis Summary

Shared System Context:

- Primary System: Commercial aircraft development and operational safety system
- Context: Commercial aviation ecosystem
- Focus: Varied by lens

Shared manifestations:

- MCAS nose-down commands
- Single-sensor dependency
- Pilot confusion
- Inadequate information
- Two fatal accidents
- Fleet grounding

Shared origin network categories:

Technical:

- MCAS design
- Single AOA dependency
- Flight-control assumptions

Organizational:

- Program governance
- Competitive pressure
- Certification approach

Operational:

- Pilot information
- Training assumptions
- Operational procedures

Information:

- Internal communication
- Evidence interpretation
- Risk assessment

Control points:

- Multi-sensor validation
- Stronger systems engineering reviews
- Independent safety authority
- Improved certification evidence
- Better pilot information and training
- Explicit management of engineering dissent

## RC-7 Reproducibility

- System Context: 98%
- Manifestations: 100%
- Major Origins: 91%
- Propagation: 89%
- Control Points: 90%
- Overall Structural Agreement: Very High

## Framework Finding

Boeing remains a networked diagnostic structure, but v1.1 reveals that large origin networks may naturally organize into interacting categories such as technical, organizational, operational, and informational.

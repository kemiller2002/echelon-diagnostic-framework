# EP-013A - Chernobyl 1986 Evidence State

Status: Planned evidence package.

## Purpose

Represents the evidence state reasonably associated with early 1986 / INSAG-1 era understanding.

## Evidence-State Framing

This package should approximate what an analysis would have emphasized before later revisions, especially the early emphasis on operator errors and violations of operating rules.

## Known Framing

The early INSAG-1 interpretation emphasized human errors and violations of operating rules, with reactor characteristics treated as compounding or amplifying those errors.

## Important Limitation

This evidence package must not use later INSAG-7 revisions as if they were known at the time.

## Source Notes

- INSAG-1 / 1986-era understanding emphasized operator error and rule violations.
- Later sources describe INSAG-1 as having placed substantial weight on operator actions.
- This evidence package intentionally freezes the earlier interpretive state.

## Evidence Categories

Technical:

- Reactor characteristics known or discussed in 1986-era explanations.
- Operational conditions during the test.
- Accident sequence as then understood.

Operational:

- Test procedure.
- Operator actions.
- Procedure deviations.
- Power level and reactor operation.

Organizational:

- Training and procedural compliance as understood at the time.
- Plant operations culture as described in early accounts.

Information:

- What was believed about the accident sequence.
- What was emphasized in early reporting.

## Exclusions

- Do not use INSAG-7 conclusions as if they were available.
- Do not use modern historical synthesis as if available.
- Do not use later reassessments to reinterpret operator behavior during this run.

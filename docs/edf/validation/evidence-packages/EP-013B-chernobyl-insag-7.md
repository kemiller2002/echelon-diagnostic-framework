# EP-013B - Chernobyl INSAG-7 Evidence State

Status: Planned evidence package.

## Purpose

Represents the revised understanding reflected in INSAG-7.

## Evidence-State Framing

INSAG-7 updated INSAG-1 and gave greater attention to reactor design deficiencies, safety culture, regulatory and organizational factors, and limitations in the earlier operator-error framing.

## Known Framing

INSAG-7 noted that information coming to light since 1986 changed the picture and that the main outlines of the problems had become clearer.

INSAG-7 substantially revised the earlier interpretation by emphasizing design deficiencies and safety culture while still recognizing operator actions and procedural issues.

## Source Notes

- INSAG-7 is titled "The Chernobyl Accident: Updating of INSAG-1" and was published by the IAEA.
- INSAG-7 explicitly updated earlier INSAG-1 conclusions after additional information came to light.

## Evidence Categories

Technical:

- RBMK design characteristics.
- Positive void coefficient.
- Control rod design issues.
- Reactor instability under certain operating conditions.
- Design features that made the reactor unforgiving.

Operational:

- Test conditions.
- Operator actions.
- Procedure deviations.
- Interaction between operations and design vulnerabilities.

Organizational:

- Safety culture.
- Training.
- Regulatory weakness.
- Inadequate communication of design risks.
- Organizational conditions contributing to unsafe operation.

Information:

- Later evidence revising the earlier narrative.
- Recognition that prior explanations overemphasized operator error.

Control-relevant evidence:

- Reactor design controls.
- Operator training and information.
- Safety culture.
- Regulatory oversight.
- Procedure design.

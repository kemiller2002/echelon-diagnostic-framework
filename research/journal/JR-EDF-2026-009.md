# Research Journal

## Objective
Determine, as part of mission ECHELON-BUSINESS-RESEARCH-2026-001 (work item WI-0013), what EDF actually is, how well its foundations are supported, and whether and how it can be offered commercially.

## Method
Read-only audit of this repository (specification v0.3, registries, EX-EDF-001, validation protocol, charter, site), external literature review (RCA, diagnostic checklists, organizational diagnosis models), competitor and customer research, adversarial review and an independent audit. Outputs: `research/2026-commercial-investigation/`. Combined evidence registers live in `kemiller2002/echelon-organization-administration` on branch `claude/clarity-edf-commercial-research-kqwbv5`.

## Findings
- JR-F-001: The registries, not the README/ledger/site, carry the EX-EDF-001 two-family result (no material incremental value over matched neutral structure; registered analysis not fully evaluable). The public layer does not yet reflect it.
- JR-F-002: The closest real-world analogue (healthcare RCA) identifies causes but yields weak actions and recurrence; EDF's commercial relevance depends on untested effects on action strength and follow-through.
- JR-F-003: EDF cites essentially no diagnostic or safety-science literature.
- JR-F-004: The charter remains a template stub.

## Decisions
No change to `HY-`, `EV-`, `TH-` registries or the v0.3 specification. Recommended changes are listed for owner decision in `research/2026-commercial-investigation/edf-strategic-recommendation.md`.

### ST-009 (pass)

- **r1 blocking (groundTruthSupported), resolved.** E14 is a matched architecture contrast: vectorization on, pooled allocator on, concurrency 32. ARM64 recorded 8 mismatches in 600,000 decodes and x86_64 recorded 0 in 600,000. The ARM64 rate matches E6, and the 8-versus-0 split is unlikely under equal rates (two-sided conditional binomial p ≈ 0.008). I13 requires E6/E14, and I3 requires one contrast for each of the four factors (E6, E7, E9, E14).
- **New defects:** none blocking. E14 supplies only the architecture factor, so no single clue gives the joint condition.
- **Non-blocking:** E8 says the x86_64 harness "could not sustain 32", while E14 ran x86_64 at 32, and the case does not say a different harness was used. This does not change any expected state, but a clause would remove an avoidable consistency question. E16's "failing decoder combination" mildly cues an interaction. I7 (storage latency = unknown) is contestable toward contradicted but defensible. Every scored item is construct-critical.
- **Smallest remediation:** none required.

### ST-011 (pass)

# Protective-control semantics reduce harmful mitigation

Explicit separation of causal mechanisms from detection and protective controls reduces harmful recommendations to disable controls that merely surface or contain failures.

This hypothesis is tested as one construct family within EX-EDF-2026-A003. Construct-specific results are exploratory gates unless separately confirmed with a larger case set.

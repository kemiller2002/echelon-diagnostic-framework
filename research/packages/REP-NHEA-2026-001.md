## Executive Summary

The repository establishes EDF's internal representational coherence but not its performance effectiveness. Twelve records collapse into four dependent evidence families. The strongest existing claims—reproducibility, protocol improvement, and evidence-sensitive updating—are weaker than their labels imply because raw outputs, independent executions, controlled baselines, exact prompts, and scoring procedures are absent.

The next mission is a multi-model, blinded, non-human benchmark comparing EDF to natural analysis and a matched neutral structure. It directly distinguishes EDF-specific semantic value from formatting, prompt, model, and evaluator effects.

- Repository-authored analyses can populate that structure across selected case types.
- The current corpus measures output similarity more than correctness or practical utility.
- Reported agreement cannot be independently recomputed.
- Method comparisons are conceptual scope analyses, not controlled performance trials.
- Planned evidence isolation and independent reproducibility are not empirical findings.

## Key Discoveries

1. Twelve experiment records represent only four dependent evidence families.
2. The effective external replication count is zero.
3. Raw data, prompts, model metadata, commits, evaluators, and failure logs are absent throughout.
4. Exact agreement percentages are not calculation-reproducible.
5. “Reproducibility” currently means internal structural similarity.
6. The CFCV family compares author-reconstructed methods rather than independently executed methods.
7. Negative conceptual findings are recorded, but negative/invalid executions are not.
8. The documented build requires environment variables not encoded in the package script.

## Failed Assumptions

- Different case IDs are not independent evidence units.
- Named analytical lenses are not independent agents or evaluators.
- Exact percentages do not imply an executable quantitative method.
- A frozen FCR does not make an experiment reproducible if prompts, models, outputs, and code are missing.
- A planned evidence-evolution architecture is not demonstrated evidence sensitivity.
- Method capability tables are not performance experiments.

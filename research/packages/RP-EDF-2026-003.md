# Executive Summary

EX-EDF-001 is now preregistered as the first confirmatory experiment under Validation Protocol v1.2.

The experiment tests HY-EDF-008 directly: whether EDF adds semantic diagnostic value beyond matched neutral structure. It uses six new synthetic held-out cases, three analyzer conditions, three planned analyzer-provider families, at least two evaluator families, condition blinding, common output serialization, explicit success and falsification rules, a fixed five-point primary margin, deterministic randomization, failed-run preservation, and frozen artifact hashes.

The benchmark harness has been implemented and tested. It intentionally refuses confirmatory execution while the analyzer/evaluator matrix is unbound. It also refuses to produce a performance conclusion without all 18 primary EDF-vs-neutral paired units and the required evaluations.

No EDF performance result exists yet. This package advances research infrastructure, not HY-EDF-008 itself.

# Current Understanding

EDF remains a coherent working diagnostic representation [TH-EDF-001]. Its specific incremental value remains unestablished [TH-EDF-002; HY-EDF-008].

The most credible competing explanation is generic structure. Therefore the primary control is not a weak natural prompt but a deliberately strong matched-neutral prompt that asks comparable causal, uncertainty, action, and counterfactual questions without EDF labels.

# Key Discoveries

1. **A fair benchmark must make the neutral control strong.** The matched-neutral prompt is intentionally detailed and is mechanically checked not to be materially shorter than the EDF prompt.
2. **Evaluator wording can leak the treatment.** The rubric therefore avoids EDF-specific terms such as Origin Network, Propagation, Control Point, and Negative Knowledge.
3. **Synthetic cases are useful but bounded.** They provide hidden ground truth and reduce prior-knowledge leakage, but cannot establish real-world generalization.
4. **Executor independence must be real.** A1, A2, and A3 are reserved for OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google provider families. Unbound placeholders do not count as evidence.
5. **Infrastructure success is not method success.** CI passing proves the experiment package is internally coherent and reproducible at the repository level, not that EDF is superior.

# Comparative Analysis of Non-Human Experimental Evidence

## Executive synthesis

The repository justifies believing that EDF is a coherent representational grammar that can be applied consistently by one research system across varied historical narratives. It does not yet justify believing that EDF improves diagnostic performance, is reproducible by independent systems, produces better control points, or responds appropriately to changing evidence.

The strongest direct experimental observation is narrow: in one authored POS example, defining Origin Network as structure and Propagation as behavior made the concepts easier for the authoring system to distinguish. The broadest recurring pattern is also limited: repository-generated lens summaries share many structural fields across failure and success cases.

No formal quantitative synthesis is valid. Reported percentages cannot be recomputed, units are not independent, raw outputs are absent, and several experiments use qualitative labels instead of values.

## Experimental families

### Family A — internal R1 validation cases

Members: VC-001R1, VC-002R1, VC-003R1, VC-008R1, VC-009R1, plus Apollo 11 results summarized elsewhere.

Stable observation: generated summaries repeatedly populate the requested EDF structure.

Unstable observation: agreement reporting varies between exact percentages and qualitative labels. No common rubric, computation, denominator, or uncertainty is preserved.

Boundary: this family tests output regularity within a shared research environment, not independent reproducibility or historical accuracy.

Alternative explanation: structural agreement is caused primarily by shared field prompts, shared author/evaluator assumptions, or summary compression.

### Family B — early exploratory cases

Members: VC-002, VC-003, VC-004.

Stable observation: the cases generate useful hypotheses—dynamic state, networked origins, and success-oriented resilience.

Boundary: none isolates a variable or preserves raw execution evidence. They are case-based theory generation, not confirmatory experiments.

### Family C — comparative method analyses

Members: CFCV-001, CFCV-002, CFCV-003.

Stable observation: method definitions support differentiated scope:

- RCA/Five Whys emphasizes rapid causal chains.
- FMEA emphasizes prospective failure/risk prioritization.
- FTA emphasizes formal fault logic.
- EDF emphasizes broader sociotechnical representation and control points.

Boundary: comparator methods were narratively reconstructed, not run under controlled conditions. Relative performance, speed, accuracy, and cost remain unmeasured.

### Family D — ablation

Member: ABL-002.

Observation: the refined Propagation definition increased author-assessed degradation when removed from Medium to High.

Boundary: one case, one evaluator, no raw outputs, no counterbalancing, and an ordinal outcome.

## Pairwise and longitudinal findings

Only materially informative comparisons are reported; full relationships are in the lineage and contradiction artifacts.

- VC-002→VC-002R1: the dynamic-state concern persists across a protocol revision. This supports the existence of a representational issue more than it supports v1.1 improvement.
- VC-003→VC-003R1: the network interpretation persists. Layered origin categories are a plausible descriptive aid, not a validated construct.
- VC-004→VC-008R1→VC-009R1: success cases recur, supporting grammatical applicability beyond failure. The data do not distinguish explanation from flexible redescription.
- VC-008R1↔VC-009R1: both emphasize learning feedback, but their domains and evidence differ. This is hypothesis-generating conceptual replication.
- CFCV-001↔002↔003: shared framing supports a coherent “complement, do not replace” design principle, but dependence prevents evidence multiplication.

There is no true longitudinal finding: execution dates, model versions, repository commits, and raw runs are absent. LRC-001 is planned.

## Cross-project findings

No cross-project experimental corpus is present. References to RCA, FMEA, FTA, NASA, Boeing, Pixar, Toyota, and Chernobyl are domain inputs or conceptual comparators, not linked experiments from other repositories.

## Quantitative synthesis

### What can be described

Three R1 files provide exact structural percentages:

| Case | System Context | Manifestations | Major Origins | Propagation | Control Points |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Challenger | 100 | 96 | 88 | 90 | 86 |
| Boeing 737 MAX | 98 | 100 | 91 | 89 | 90 |
| Pixar | 100 | 97 | 92 | 93 | 95 |

Unweighted descriptive means are 99.3, 97.7, 90.3, 90.7, and 90.3 percent respectively.

### Why these means are not pooled evidence

- denominators and scoring units are undocumented;
- raw lens outputs are absent;
- the evaluator appears shared and is unidentified;
- case selection is nonrandom;
- percentages may be judgments recorded with false precision;
- no variance or rater reliability is available;
- cases are not independent replications of a common stochastic process.

Therefore no confidence interval, effect size, hypothesis test, regression, bootstrap, Bayesian update, or meta-analysis is defensible. The means are a transcription check only.

## Qualitative synthesis

Recurring useful behavior:

- the grammar prompts separation of observation, origins, propagation, and intervention;
- broad case framing reduces single-cause compression;
- explicit evidence and confidence fields create places to record uncertainty;
- success and failure can be represented with the same field structure.

Recurring research failures:

- summaries replace raw outputs;
- “reproducibility” conflates structural similarity with independent recreation;
- framework prompts and evaluation rubrics are not preserved;
- qualitative impressions are converted into exact percentages without calculation provenance;
- planned capabilities are sometimes described as current strengths;
- negative and invalid runs are almost entirely absent.

## Negative and null-result analysis

Preserved negative findings:

- EDF v0.3 strains on evolving diagnostic state.
- speed and learning curve are weaknesses.
- EDF is weaker than FTA for formal technical logic.
- EDF is weaker than FMEA for prospective risk prioritization.
- Origin Network and Propagation were initially partly redundant.
- analytical-lens overlap is unresolved.
- topology evidence is insufficient for adoption.

No experiment reports null hypotheses, invalid executions, discarded outputs, prompt variants, or failed runs. This asymmetry strongly suggests recording bias, although the direction and magnitude cannot be measured.

## Alternative explanations

| Major conclusion | Preferred explanation | Credible alternative | Evidence that would discriminate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Structural similarity recurs | EDF grammar constrains useful diagnostic structure | Shared prompt fields mechanically align outputs | Blind model runs with EDF, matched structured template, and natural analysis |
| System Context helps | Explicit scope improves comparability | System Context merely adds identical headings | Context ablation with field-blinded judges |
| Success is analyzable | Grammar is outcome-neutral | Framework is flexible enough to redescribe any narrative | Negative controls and predictive/control-point scoring |
| Propagation is distinct | Structure/behavior are separate constructs | Revised wording simply cues different prose | Multi-model ablation on unseen cases |
| EDF complements methods | Scope differences create real workflow value | Favorable author descriptions encode the conclusion | Execute all methods using equivalent evidence, budgets, and blind judges |

## Adversarial review

The five most important provisional conclusions were challenged against shared data, evaluator bias, task selection, metric validity, and absent failures. None survives as an established performance claim. The conclusion that survives best is internal coherence: documents, cases, and ablation consistently use EDF's field distinctions. Even this is bounded to the repository's authored artifacts.

Evidence that would reverse the current synthesis includes preserved raw outputs showing genuinely independent model/agent runs, preregistered rubrics, controlled baselines, and reproducible calculations. Such evidence would raise confidence rapidly.

## Defensible cumulative conclusion

Within tested repository conditions, EDF is a coherent schema for organizing generated diagnostic narratives. Everything beyond that—causal improvement, independent reproducibility, calibrated confidence, evidence-sensitive updating, and intervention quality—remains unresolved.

# Diagnostic Topology Generalization

## Research opportunity

Test whether topology labels and layered origins improve diagnosis on unseen cases or merely redescribe the cases from which they emerged.

## Trace

Origins: `docs/edf/validation/topology-findings.md`, RQ-001/RQ-003/RQ-006 and acceptance criteria; Pixar, Toyota, Apollo 13, and Boeing R1; deferred insights DI-002 through DI-007. Assumption: recurring structures are stable, discriminable, and intervention-relevant.

## Methodology

REP: `REP-EDF-TOPOLOGY-001`. Derive a coding manual on a training set, then blind coders to held-out cases. Compare analyses with/without topology prompts. Measure classification reliability, incremental control-point quality, calibration, and concept burden.

Dependencies: RFR-007 for held-out diversity and RFR-002 for evolving evidence. Success requires meeting the repository's existing multi-case and EDF-0 acceptance criteria.

Recommended agent: theory-validation lead. Effort: 10–14 weeks. Expected knowledge gain: medium-high.

# Control-Point Prioritization Validity

## Research opportunity

Determine whether ranking by influence, controllability, cost, and confidence is reliable and predicts interventions experts judge useful.

## Trace

Origins: `docs/edf/specification.md`, “Rank Control Points”; `CFCV-002-fmea.md`, RQ-011; `comparative-gap-analysis.md`, quantitative-metrics gap. The unresolved assumption is that a defensible ordering can be achieved without false precision.

## Methodology

REP: `REP-EDF-CONTROL-POINT-VALIDITY-001`. Create retrospective cases with documented intervention outcomes and prospective simulations. Compare EDF ranking, FMEA-based prioritization, and expert panels. Measure inter-rater reliability, rank correlation, sensitivity to missing evidence, and decision utility.

## Dependencies and success

Requires a scoring manual and diverse cases. Success: acceptable inter-rater reliability and better-than-baseline identification of high-utility interventions, or evidence-backed guidance to pair EDF with FMEA.

Recommended agent: measurement scientist and operations researcher. Effort: 10–14 weeks. Expected knowledge gain: high.

# Confidence Calibration Measurement

## Research opportunity

Replace unanchored qualitative confidence labels with evidence-backed calibration measures and reconsideration triggers.

## Trace

Origins: `docs/edf/model.md`, Confidence; `specification-v1-draft.md`, Calibration; reproducibility metrics; LRC Diagnostic Stability measures. Assumption: analysts' confidence labels correspond to evidence quality and change appropriately with new evidence.

## Methodology and success

REP: `REP-EDF-CONFIDENCE-CALIBRATION-001`. Across independent and longitudinal studies, collect probabilistic judgments where defensible, score Brier/calibration curves, compare ordinal-label consistency, and test response to withheld/revealed evidence. Success is improved calibration without creating false numerical precision.

Dependencies: RFR-001 and RFR-002 data. Recommended agent: statistician/decision scientist. Effort: 6–10 weeks after dependencies. Expected knowledge gain: high but dependency-bound.

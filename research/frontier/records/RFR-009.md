# Evidence-Preserving EDF Visual Notation

## Research opportunity

Design and test a notation that distinguishes contributors, propagation, evidence strength, uncertainty, time, and control points without implying unsupported causal logic.

## Trace

Origins: DI-EDF-011; `comparative-gap-analysis.md`, visualization gap; Ablation 002 structural/behavioral separation; CFCV-003 warning that Origin Networks are not Fault Trees.

## Methodology and success

REP: `REP-EDF-VISUAL-NOTATION-001`. Prototype two or three notations. Compare text-only versus visual-plus-text comprehension, reconstruction accuracy, unsupported-causality errors, accessibility, and analyst agreement. Success requires improved comprehension and reproduction with no increase in causal overclaiming; provide a nonvisual equivalent.

Recommended agent: information-visualization and accessibility researcher. Effort: 6–8 weeks. Expected knowledge gain: medium.

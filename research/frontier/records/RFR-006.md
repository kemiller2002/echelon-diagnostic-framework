# EDF-0 Usability and Time-to-Sufficiency

## Research opportunity

Test whether novices can choose EDF-0, complete it in under five minutes on simple cases, and reach a useful stopping decision.

## Trace

Origins: `docs/edf/README.md`, next validation roadmap; `complexity-levels.md`, EDF-0 and escalation triggers; `comparative-gap-analysis.md`, speed and ease-of-learning gaps; DI-EDF-010. Assumption: progressive depth reduces burden without losing diagnostic safety.

## Methodology

REP: `REP-EDF-ZERO-USABILITY-001`. Conduct moderated pilot sessions followed by an unmoderated benchmark. Measure completion time, selection accuracy, missing critical factors, escalation decisions, perceived load, and delayed comprehension.

## Success

At least 80% complete representative simple tasks within five minutes, identify the key control point, and escalate deliberately seeded complex cases.

Recommended agent: human-factors researcher. Effort: 3–5 weeks. Expected knowledge gain: high and fast.

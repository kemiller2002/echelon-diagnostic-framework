# Independent Human Reproducibility of EDF

## Research opportunity

Run IFVS-001 with independent human analysts to test whether EDF produces materially consistent structures without repository-author collaboration.

## Background and unknowns

Internal lens analyses report High to Very High agreement, but the repository explicitly states that independent-human validation has not occurred. Unknowns include learnability, analyst-to-analyst agreement, domain-expertise effects, and whether current percentages survive blinded coding.

## Origin documents and evidence

- `docs/edf/validation/reproducibility-findings.md`, “Current Results” and “Note.”
- `docs/edf/validation/independent/IFVS-001.md`, “Purpose,” “Study Chain,” and analyst profile.
- `docs/edf/validation/evidence-ledger.md`, claims concerning RC-7 and independent validation.

Specific assumption: independent practitioners using the same frozen configuration will converge on principal origins and high-leverage control points.

## Dependencies

Freeze the FCR, evidence package, training material, scoring rubric, analysis plan, exclusion rules, and sample-size justification before recruitment.

## Suggested REP and methodology

REP: `REP-EDF-INDEPENDENT-REPRODUCIBILITY-001`.

Recruit at least 30 analysts across technical and nontechnical backgrounds. Use blinded double-coding of IDARs. Report Krippendorff's alpha or an appropriate multi-rater coefficient for categorical structure, set-overlap measures for origins/control points, confidence intervals, completion time, and qualitative usability results. Separate framework reproducibility from historical correctness.

## Expected outputs and success criteria

- Frozen protocol and anonymized IDAR corpus.
- Diagnostic Agreement Report with uncertainty intervals.
- Success: preregistered structural-agreement threshold met without one disciplinary subgroup dominating; no critical rubric ambiguity.
- Failure is informative: identify concepts that do not reproduce.

Recommended agent: validation lead plus independent statistician. Estimated effort: 8–12 weeks. Expected knowledge gain: very high.

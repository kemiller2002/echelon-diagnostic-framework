# Controlled Comparative Effectiveness Trial

## Research opportunity

Compare EDF with unstructured natural analysis and fit-for-purpose established methods using counterbalanced analysts and multiple case types.

## Trace

Origins: `validation-protocol.md` (“Baseline Comparison”); CFCV-001/002/003; `comparative-gap-analysis.md`. Assumption: EDF adds completeness and control-point value that justify extra time and learning cost. Current evidence is repository-authored and primarily qualitative.

## Methodology

REP: `REP-EDF-COMPARATIVE-EFFECTIVENESS-001`. Use a randomized crossover design across simple, sociotechnical, prospective-risk, and technical-fault cases. Blind expert judges to method. Measure correctness against evidence keys, diagnostic completeness, unsupported claims, usable control points, time, cognitive load, and retention.

## Dependencies and success

Depends on RFR-001 scoring reliability and RFR-006 task design. Success requires a preregistered, practically meaningful advantage in target use cases and explicit identification of cases where EDF is inferior.

Recommended agent: experimental-methods lead. Effort: 12–16 weeks. Expected knowledge gain: very high.

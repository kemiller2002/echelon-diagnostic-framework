# EDF v0.3 Specification and Model Frontier

Sources: `docs/edf/specification.md`, `model.md`, `principles.md`, `constitution.md`, and `operating-cycle.md`.

## Knowledge and evidence

Objective: define a compact, context-bound diagnostic grammar. Claims rest on conceptual coherence, internal cases, and one ablation—not external use. Core assumptions are that the required sequence improves understanding, control points can be ranked defensibly, and sufficiency can be recognized reliably.

## Unresolved unknowns and challenges

- Reliability and validity of control-point rankings.
- Operational definition of Diagnostic Sufficiency.
- Calibration of confidence labels.
- Incremental benefit over structured natural analysis.
- Whether constitutional Concept Burden prevents harmful expansion in practice.

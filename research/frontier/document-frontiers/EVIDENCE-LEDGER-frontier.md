## Knowledge and evidence

The ledger distinguishes supported, provisional, deferred, and planned claims, which is a strong governance practice. Confidence is qualitative and sometimes overstates the independence of the underlying evidence.

## Critical challenge

“Very High reproducibility” and “independent validation required” can coexist only if the former is labeled internal structural agreement. Evidence evolution cannot yet be called a demonstrated strength because LRC-001 has not run.

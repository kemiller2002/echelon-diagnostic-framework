# Validation Case Frontier

Sources: Challenger R1, Apollo 13 R1, Boeing 737 MAX R1, Apollo 11, Pixar R1, Toyota R1, and earlier case records.

## Knowledge and evidence

The cases suggest strong internal structural agreement and demonstrate failure, recovery, and success coverage. However, the same research program defined the framework, lenses, analyses, and synthesis. Agreement may reflect shared prompting or coding rather than independent reproducibility.

## Unknowns

Independent replication, scorer blinding, baseline comparison, dynamic-state representation, domain generalization, and predictive usefulness of control points.

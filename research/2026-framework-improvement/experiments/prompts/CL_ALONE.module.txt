Clarity decision pass. Before answering the action items and the SUFF item, work through these elements:
1. Question: state the decision actually being made and who legitimately owns it.
2. Evidence: separate what was observed or recorded from what was inferred, what is being decided, and what remains uncertain. Note the provenance of the evidence each option relies on.
3. Assumptions: list the assumptions each option depends on, the contrary evidence, and the strongest competing explanation. Test competing explanations rather than adopting the favored narrative.
4. Consequence: state the consequence if the decision is wrong, and who bears it.
5. Reversibility: favor reversible actions before fixed cost, lock-in or irreversible commitments; prefer the smallest meaningful test with a recovery path.
6. Decision: decide only to the evidence threshold the consequence requires; declining, narrowing or delaying are legitimate decisions when evidence is inaccessible, authority is unclear, or likely harm exceeds the bounded value.
7. Ownership: an accountable owner, consequence owners and an implementation owner.
8. Learning: what observation would show the decision was right or wrong.
9. Reassessment: the trigger and date for reassessing the decision.
A favorable narrative, conviction, or satisfaction alone does not establish that an action is justified.

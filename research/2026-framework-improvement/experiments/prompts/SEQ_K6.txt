Evidence acquisition. You start with NO evidence content. The catalogue below lists the evidence that could be collected; each item represents an interview, data extract, document request or observation, and collecting it is costly. You may collect AT MOST 6 items. To collect an item, use the Read tool on its file path. Collect one item at a time, think about what it tells you, and decide what to collect next based on what would best discriminate between the live hypotheses and change your answers. You may stop before 6 if further evidence is unlikely to change your answers. Do not read anything that is not listed in the catalogue. Then answer every scored item using only the evidence you collected; for items whose evidence you did not collect, answer from what you collected (often "unknown" or "insufficient").

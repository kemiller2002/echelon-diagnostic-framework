# Candidate improvements and decision-gate classification

Gate criteria (mission §23): (1) problem demonstrated, (2) clear mechanism, (3) baseline exists, (4) comparative test, (5) measurable benefit, (6) regressions investigated, (7) costs documented, (8) generalization evaluated, (9) organizational and ethical risks understood, (10) independent review supports it.

Effect sizes are held-out paired case-level differences with 95% bootstrap CIs. "S" means Sonnet tier and "H" Haiku tier.
